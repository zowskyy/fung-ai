# NO SAND BEACH — Sequence 1: The Setup
## Beat-by-Beat Breakdown

**Sequence ID:** `nsb_seq_01_the_setup`  
**Target Duration:** 2:45 (165 seconds)  
**Theme:** Poverty, love, absence, and the cost of survival  
**Tone:** Intimate, weary, haunting (visual: muted colors, tight framing)

---

## Scene 1A: Before Dawn — Delia's Shift (0:00–0:45)

### Environment
- **Location:** Small Santa Ana apartment (1BR, worn furniture, family photos on walls)
- **Time:** 3:00 AM (darkness, single lamp)
- **Atmosphere:** Quiet, intimate, exhausted

### Characters Present
- Delia (mother, 40s, worn face, working-class hands)
- Pure (child/teen composite, sleeping)

### Beat Sequence

#### Beat 1.01: THE RETURN
- **Type:** Action + Dialogue (internal monologue)
- **Actor:** Delia
- **Action:** 
  - Delia enters apartment quietly (key in lock, door opens)
  - She sets down nurse's bag (heavily, exhausted)
  - She removes shoes, winces (feet hurt)
  - She looks at Pure sleeping on couch
- **Duration:** 15 seconds
- **Dialogue (V.O.):** "Twenty-three clients today. Mrs. Chen's hip. Mr. Rodriguez's catheter. Three hours on the 101 traffic. Still got to do Hilton prep at 6."
- **Camera:** Wide shot of apartment → Slow push into Delia's face (exhaustion) → Close-up: her knees buckling slightly
- **Visual Notes:** 
  - Color palette: cool blues, grays, single warm lamp
  - Delia's uniform is wrinkled, stained
  - Hands shake slightly (fatigue)
- **Audio Notes:**
  - Ambient: distant traffic, humming refrigerator
  - Delia's breathing (tired)
  - Footsteps (soft, careful not to wake Pure)
- **Next Beat:** 1.02 (forehead kiss)
- **Failure Path:** None (linear progression)

---

#### Beat 1.02: THE RITUAL
- **Type:** Action + Emotional Moment
- **Actor:** Delia, Pure (sleeping)
- **Action:**
  - Delia kneels beside couch where Pure sleeps
  - She brushes hair from Pure's forehead gently
  - She kisses forehead (tender, long—this is her anchor)
  - Her hand lingers on Pure's chest
  - She closes eyes, whispers prayer/affirmation
- **Duration:** 12 seconds
- **Dialogue (V.O.):** "You're gonna make it, baby. You're gonna have better than this. Better than me."
- **Camera:** Close-up on Delia's face → Pull back to show her small figure over sleeping child → Intimate two-shot
- **Visual Notes:**
  - Lighting: single lamp creates stark shadows
  - Delia's face softens (all exhaustion drops away in this moment)
  - Pure is peaceful, safe
  - Contrast: mother's pain vs. child's innocence
- **Audio Notes:**
  - Silence underneath dialogue
  - Heartbeat (Delia's?)
  - Faint crickets outside
- **Next Beat:** 1.03 (coffee)
- **Failure Path:** None

---

#### Beat 1.03: COFFEE MACHINE
- **Type:** Action (physical struggle)
- **Actor:** Delia
- **Action:**
  - Delia moves to kitchen (5 steps, tiny space)
  - She fills coffee maker (mechanical, automatic)
  - Waits for drip (impatient, leaning against counter)
  - She stares at kitchen clock: 3:47 AM
  - Takes first sip (burns her mouth, doesn't care)
- **Duration:** 10 seconds
- **Dialogue (V.O.):** "No time. Never time. Just make it to 6 AM."
- **Camera:** Static wide shot of kitchen → Slow dolly on clock → Close-up: coffee cup → Her face over rim (thousand-yard stare)
- **Visual Notes:**
  - Kitchen is spotless (she takes pride, even exhausted)
  - Clock is the focal point (time is enemy)
  - Delia in profile against fridge light
  - Colors: harsh fluorescent overhead (she turns it off after coffee)
- **Audio Notes:**
  - Coffee drip (rhythmic, meditative)
  - Clock ticking (slight overdub for emphasis)
  - Fridge hum
- **Next Beat:** 1.04 (prep for Hilton)
- **Failure Path:** None

#### Beat 1.04: THE UNIFORM CHANGE
- **Type:** Action (montage compression)
- **Actor:** Delia
- **Action:**
  - Delia moves to bedroom (closes door quietly)
  - She removes scrubs (reveals bruises on arm, tape on shoulder)
  - She puts on Hilton housekeeping uniform (different, cleaner, yet same exhaustion)
  - Quick mirror check (straightens herself)
  - Sets phone alarm for 8:30 PM (next shift start)
- **Duration:** 8 seconds
- **Dialogue (V.O.):** "Hilton pays better. Hotel housekeeper, 6 to 2. Then cafeteria at 3. Just keep moving."
- **Camera:** Series of tight shots (scrubs off → arm bruise visible → new uniform on → mirror reflection)
- **Visual Notes:**
  - Bruises tell story of physical toll
  - Mirror shows Delia doesn't recognize herself
  - Uniform is her armor
  - Bedroom is sparse (bed, one drawer, nothing personal)
- **Audio Notes:**
  - Clothing rustles
  - Bedspring creak
  - Whisper of alarm being set
- **Next Beat:** 1.05 (departure)
- **Failure Path:** None

---

#### Beat 1.05: THE DEPARTURE
- **Type:** Action + Dialogue
- **Actor:** Delia
- **Action:**
  - Delia moves back to living room (Pure still sleeping)
  - She looks at Pure one more time
  - She gathers bags (nurse bag + work tote)
  - Opens door
  - Pauses in doorway (just holds the frame, no words)
  - Steps out, door closes quietly
  - Through window: her car starts, headlights sweep across apartment
  - Car leaves
- **Duration:** 10 seconds
- **Dialogue (V.O.):** "I love you, Pure. I love you so much it scares me."
- **Camera:** Wide shot from Pure's POV (sleeping) → Focus pulls to Delia in doorway → Follows her to car through window → Car leaves (reverse angle: Delia's face in rearview mirror)
- **Visual Notes:**
  - Doorway framing emphasizes her absence (what will fill that space?)
  - Window reflection shows her exhaustion
  - Car is old, reliable (not fancy)
  - Dawn is not yet breaking
- **Audio Notes:**
  - Door closes (soft click)
  - Keys jingle
  - Car door opens/closes
  - Engine turns over
  - Radio static as she drives away
  - Fade to silence
- **Next Beat:** 1.06 (transition to Pure's awakening)
- **Failure Path:** None

- **Type:** Action (Child's awakening)
- **Actor:** Pure

  - Rubs eyes, disoriented (is this home? where's mom?)

- **Visual Notes:**

  - Toy/book on floor (he's still a kid, but no one plays with him)
  - Clock is always visible (time structure his life)
- **Audio Notes:**

  - Clock ticking

- **Visual Notes:**

  - One chair is gone (literal absence)
  - Pure's face shows neither sadness nor contentment—just acceptance
  - Window behind him shows street (neighbor kids walking to school with parents)
- **Audio Notes:**

#### Beat 1.09: MARCUS ARRIVES
- **Type:** Action + Dialogue (Tension introduction)
- **Actor:** Marcus (brother, early 30s), Pure
- **Action:**
  - Door opens (Marcus has key)
  - Marcus enters, already dressed for work (button-up shirt, tired face)
  - He stops when he sees Pure
  - Marcus: "You eating breakfast? Where's Mom?"
  - Pure (mouth full): "Work."
  - Marcus shakes head (familiar frustration)
  - Marcus moves to fridge, grabs something
  - He looks at Pure like he's solving a math problem
- **Duration:** 12 seconds
- **Dialogue:**
  - Marcus: "Pure, it's 7:15. School starts at 8."
  - Pure: "I know."
  - Marcus: "Did Mom leave money for lunch?"
  - Pure: "Yeah. On the counter."
  - Marcus (V.O., internal): "He shouldn't have to know about money yet. He shouldn't have to know she's not coming back today."
- **Camera:** Marcus's POV entering (surprise/frustration) → Two-shot of brothers (height/age difference emphasized) → Close-up: Marcus's face (calculating, resentful) → Pure eating (oblivious/resigned)
- **Visual Notes:**
  - Marcus looks older than his age (responsibility)
  - Contrast: Marcus in work clothes, Pure in school clothes, empty chairs
  - Marcus's eyes scan the apartment (checking condition, checking on Pure)
  - Tension in his shoulders (he didn't want to be parent, but here he is)
- **Audio Notes:**
  - Door opens/closes
  - Footsteps (heavier than Delia's)
  - Fridge opens (light hum, items inside)
  - Their voices (tired, routine)
  - Cereal bowl clink
- **Next Beat:** 1.10 (Marcus's resentment)
- **Failure Path:** None

- **Time:** Afternoon recess
- **Atmosphere:** Longing, exclusion, class awareness

#### Beat 1.13: THE BEACH CONVERSATION
- **Type:** Dialogue + Visual metaphor
- **Actor:** Pure (15 years old now), Two Classmates
- **Action:**
  - Pure and two kids sitting on bench at school
  - Classmate 1: "My family's going to Huntington Beach this weekend. My dad's taking his boat."
  - Classmate 2: "We have a beach house in Newport. We're there every summer."
  - Pure is silent, listening
  - Classmate 1 (to Pure): "Where does your family go?"

  - Wide shot: bench, three kids (Pure isolated in frame)

#### Beat 1.14: EARL'S GHOST (Flashback)
- **Type:** Memory / Flashback (Visual transition)
- **Actor:** Pure (child, age 7), Earl (father, alive—memory)
- **Action:**
  - Scene shifts (visual/audio cue: clock becomes wavy, underwater effect)
  - We're in memory now
  - Pure is 7 years old, smaller, innocent
  - Earl (handsome, street presence but loving) holds Pure's hand
  - Earl: "One day, mijo. One day I'ma take you to that beach. We gonna walk in that sand, feel the sun, and you gonna know you can have anything."

- **Camera:** 
  - School bench (real time) → Wavy transition → Memory (Earl's face, loving)

## Production Notes

### Scene List for Asset Creation
1. Santa Ana apartment (interior, night & morning)
2. Kitchen (tight, functional)
3. Bedroom (Delia's, sparse)
4. School playground (afternoon light)
5. School building (establishing)
6. Aerial Santa Ana (drone shot, sunset)

### Character Sprites Needed
1. Delia (40s, exhausted, home health uniform + Hilton uniform)
2. Pure (7 years old—memory, 15 years old—present)
3. Marcus (early 30s, button-up shirt, carrying burden)
4. Earl (father, handsome, street presence—memory only)
5. Classmate 1 & 2 (extras, privileged appearance)

### Animation States
- **Delia:** entering (tired), kissing (tender), changing (routine), leaving (resigned)
- **Pure:** sleeping, waking, eating, sitting (alone), standing, walking, remembering
- **Marcus:** entering (checking), looking (resentful), paying (reluctant care), leaving
- **Earl:** (memory only) kneeling, holding, promising, vanishing

### Visual Effects
- Memory transition (wavy, color shift to sepia)
- Clock time distortion (during memory)
- Gunshot (audio, brief)
- Silhouette (final embrace)
- Title card overlay

### Dialogue/VO Distribution
- Narrator (Adult Pure, mature voice): Frames major beats, provides context
- Delia (V.O., tired): Her internal monologue during morning
- Marcus (V.O., resentful): His buried anger during photo moment
- Young Pure (V.O., child voice): His innocence/acceptance during morning
- Classmates (dialogue): School scene conversation
- Earl (dialogue): Memory scene only

### Tone & Color Palette
- **Pre-dawn (3-6 AM):** Cool blues, grays, single warm lamp (Delia's presence = warmth)
- **Morning (7-8 AM):** Pale light, dust particles, muted greens/blues (independence = coldness)
- **Afternoon (school):** Hard sunlight, shadows, chain-link silver (shame = harsh)
- **Memory (Earl):** Sepia tones, warm amber, soft focus (love = warmth, but fading)
- **Dusk (sunset):** Gold/orange over ocean, dark apartment (reunion = only warmth)

### Key Emotional Beats (Non-Negotiable)
- ✅ Delia's forehead kiss (mother's love as anchor)
- ✅ Marcus's silent resentment (brother's buried pain)
- ✅ Money envelope (care as sacrifice, not sentimentality)
